# What is sourced, and what is our own rule

Use this when a judge asks "where does that number come from?". Be exact about what is verified and what is a default.

## Sourced
| Item | Value | Source | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold storage limit | 5 C or below | Dubai Municipality: external circulars on health and safety (food delivery); Food Code 2.0 (draft v4, 2020); Gulf News report on Dubai Municipality's charity fridge requirements | Check the current official text before real use |
| Meal weight | 0.54 kg (1.2 pounds) per meal | Convention used by Feeding America and ReFED (ReFED "Meals recovered" methodology) | A US convention, not UAE specific |
| CO2e per kg of food | 2.5 kg | Derived from FAO (2013) Food wastage footprint: about 3.3 Gt CO2e a year for about 1.3 Gt edible food wasted, roughly 2.5 kg per kg | A global average excluding land-use change. The real saving depends on the food. Say "estimate" |
| UAE food waste goal | cut food waste 50% by 2030 | UAE national strategy, as cited earlier in this project | Re-verify before quoting |

## Our own rules (say so plainly)
- **Safe-window hours per food category** (for example 6 hours for cooked chilled food) and the 24 hour window for sealed chilled packs: BARAKAT's conservative defaults, not Dubai Municipality figures. Confirm with the authority before real use.
- **ESG points** (5 per meal): a BARAKAT scoring rule, not an external standard.
- **Photo reading confidence threshold** (75%): a design choice; below it a coordinator decides.
- **Volunteer speeds, offer times (6 and 15 minutes), 25 km radius**: design parameters of the prototype.

## Claims to avoid
- "Certified", "approved by Dubai Municipality", "audited" or "guaranteed safe".
- Real-world totals from sample data. Say "sample data" every time you show numbers.
